Abstract Kit

Everything you need to write the SEED abstract yourself: facts you may quote, how to phrase them safely, a notes-only skeleton, and the questions the jury will throw at you. Due 22 Nov 2026, under 1,000 words, problem → fix → why it matters.

Why there is no draft here: SEED requires the abstract to be written by you and checks for AI-written text. The team has deliberately given you notes, facts and rules, not sentences.

How to use it (about 2 hours)

  1. Skeleton: read the budget table and the MUST bullets. Write the Fix section first, in your own words.
  2. Fact card: pull external numbers only from here, with the qualifier in the same row.
  3. Terms: use one name per concept (e.g. "suspend", not "quarantine"; "record", not "log").
  4. Guardrails: run the 12-point self-check in §4 on your draft.
  5. Save your draft as abstract/DRAFT-R1.md (or paste it to me). The team reviews it with comments only, in two rounds, before 22 Nov.
  6. Jury Q&A: not needed for the abstract. Read it before the finale.

The frame in one breath (notes)

  • Problem: the brief asks to undo one agent "without discarding" others' work; that is undefined once honest agents read the bad write.
  • Fix: every write has an author, every read a source; identify, contain and recover are three queries on one graph.
  • Payoff: selective undo of landed writes plus repair of their readers.
  • Why it matters: an agent's own log is testimony; we keep evidence the agent cannot forge, and score recovery against a no-fault run.
