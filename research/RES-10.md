## Headline findings (things the team must know)

1. **Who&When 53.5% / 14.2% is real, but it is not one method.** It is in the abstract, and I can reproduce it from Table 1 as an unweighted mean of the four cells (my arithmetic, not stated in the paper): agent-level 53.5% = mean(54.33, 55.17, 51.12, 53.44), all from **All-at-once**; step-level 14.2% = mean(25.51, 7.02, 15.31, 8.77), all from **Step-by-step**. So no single method reaches both numbers. All GPT-4o. The abstract's phrase "the best method" is loose. Never write "one method achieves 53.5% and 14.2%".
2. **Two sets of Who&When numbers are in circulation.** FAMAS Table 1 re-reports the same baselines weighted by log count (best LLM total: 54.59% agent-level, 19.68% step/action-level, both with ground truth, different methods). A jury member may see either. Pick one, say which, and give the setting.
3. **Cordon's 45/45 vs 14/45 is verified (Tier B) but is the authors' own 45-case suite, and the 14/45 comes from the authors' own "strategy adapters derived from" existing defenses, not the real products.** Must be worded that way.
4. **State Footprints (Mraz et al.) is close prior art to our idea** (nested transaction IDs, abort one task's writes plus the tasks that read them). It is a vision paper; the rollback is not implemented or measured. Aarav should know it exists; the jury may. Our differentiator has to be that we build and measure it for per-agent identity, containment and rollback.
5. **DeltaBox discrepancy:** RES-06 cites v3 dated 13 Nov 2026 with a different title; the PDF I fetched is v2, 8 Jun 2026, titled "Scaling Stateful AI Agents with Millisecond-Level Sandbox Checkpoint/Rollback". Cite the arXiv ID and re-check the title on the abs page right before submission.
6. **Venues:** Cordon, CHIEF, FAMAS, Atomix, GoEx, DeltaBox, MAST: arXiv preprints, no accepted venue confirmed. Do not write a venue for them. Who&When is ICML 2025 (spotlight). State Footprints is formatted for PVLDB Vol. 20 (2027) but acceptance is not stated.

## Claims to DROP or reword (failed verification or too strong)

1. **"One method gets 53.5% agent-level and 14.2% step-level"** (Who&When). The two numbers come from two different methods (all-at-once vs step-by-step) averaged over four cells. Reword per row 1.
2. **MAST "94%" as a standalone reliability figure.** It is label-level accuracy of a few-shot o1 annotator on a held-out set, with recall 0.77 and kappa 0.77. Use kappa. Drop "NeurIPS" for MAST.
3. **Cordon "EuroSys '27"** and any wording that implies it was accepted. Also drop any sentence implying Cordon beat real defense products; the 14/45 baselines are the authors' derived adapters on the authors' own suite.
4. **State Footprints "PVLDB 2027" as a venue, and any claim it demonstrates undo or rollback.** It proposes, it does not implement or measure rollback. Do not cite it as evidence rollback works; cite it as prior art on framing.
5. **"Atomix beats checkpoint-rollback" and "frontier gating improves success under speculation".** Overlapping CIs on 2 of 3 real benchmarks; speculation success 77.3 vs 77.7. Also drop "Mohammadi et al." and the 29 May 2026 date unless checked on the abs page.
6. **DeltaBox as "selective" or "per-agent" rollback.** It is whole-sandbox/branch-level, no network rollback.
7. **GoEx quantitative claims.** None exist in the text I read.
8. **"CHIEF is state of the art across the board".** FAMAS still wins hand-crafted step-level with ground truth (41.38 vs 29.31), and CHIEF uses DeepSeek-V3.2 rather than GPT-4o.
9. **Using FAMAS as an online detector.** It needs 20 re-executions and about 105 min per attribution on average.
10. **Quoting two "53.5" numbers.** Atomix's tau-bench Tx-Full success is also 53.5%. If both appear, readers will conflate them.
11. Any venue for FAMAS, CHIEF, Atomix, GoEx, DeltaBox, Cordon, MAST: not confirmed, do not state one.
